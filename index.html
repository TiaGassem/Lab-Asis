Scientific Decision Assistant

Evidence-Based Guidance

This does not invent science. It reads your saved evidence and points to the next strongest action.
  • Uses your saved protocols, notes, tasks, failures, results, calendar, and papers.
  • Looks for gaps, repeated problems, missing evidence, and unfinished workflow steps.
  • Best for planning the next lab action, not for replacing expert supervision or medical advice.
