Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lab Asis handles information. Lab Asis is a scientific lab assistant created by Tasnim Gassem.

1. What the app stores

Lab Asis stores the information you enter, such as lab notes, protocols, task lists, timer logs, calendar events, article notes, learning notes, and failure logs.

2. Where data is stored

By default, data is stored locally in your browser on your own device. If you connect Make webhooks and Google Sheets, selected records may also be sent to the services you configure.

3. Data sharing

Lab Asis does not automatically send your data to a central server controlled by the creator. Data is only shared when you explicitly configure third-party services such as Make and Google Sheets.

4. Audio files

Voice note files are stored locally in your browser unless you separately export them or connect third-party tools.

5. Deletion and retention

You can delete records inside the app and export backups yourself. If you clear your browser data, locally stored data may be lost. If you send records to Google Sheets or Make, retention is also controlled by those services.

6. Security

Lab Asis is designed to minimize data collection by default. However, you are responsible for the security of your own device, your hosted copy of the app, and any connected services such as Google Sheets.

7. International use

If you publish or share Lab Asis internationally, you are responsible for complying with local data protection, ethics, and research confidentiality rules that apply to your users and their lab records.
